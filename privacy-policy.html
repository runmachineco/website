Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

At RunMachine, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our service.

Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways, including:

  • Personal Data: Email address, name, and other contact information you provide when signing up or contacting us.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our service, including access times, pages viewed, and the routes taken to access our service.
  • Integration Data: Data from third-party services you connect to RunMachine (e.g., Slack, Gmail, GitHub) to enable our AI-powered support features.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our service
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our service
  • Communicate with you about updates, support, and promotional materials
  • Process your transactions and manage your account
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

Our service may integrate with third-party services (such as Slack, Gmail, Microsoft Teams, GitHub, and others). These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access your personal data
  • The right to correct inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to data portability

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@runmachine.co.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

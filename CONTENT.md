## FAQ Section (Accordion)

### Q: How does runMachine access my company's data?
You connect data sources through OAuth. For GitHub, it's read-only access to repositories you specify. For Gmail, it reads forwarded customer emails. You control what's connected and can disconnect anytime.

### Q: What if the AI drafts something wrong?
Every response requires your approval before sending. The draft includes citations showing exactly which docs or code it referenced, so you can verify accuracy in seconds.

### Q: Do I need to change my workflow?
No. You forward customer emails to runMachine. Drafts appear in your inbox. You review and send. Same tools you already use.

### Q: How is this different from Zendesk AI or ChatGPT?
Generic AI tools don't know your product. runMachine searches YOUR codebase, YOUR docs, YOUR Slack—and cites specific evidence. It's not guessing; it's researching.

### Q: Is my data secure?
Yes. GDPR compliant, full encryption (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit), and we never train AI models on your data. Your content is indexed for search only.

### Q: How long does setup take?
10 minutes. Connect Gmail via OAuth, connect your GitHub repos, and forward your first email to test.

## Content Guidelines

### Voice & Tone
- Direct and confident (no hedging: "we believe", "we try to")
- Specific over vague ("under 2 minutes" not "fast")
- Engineer-friendly but accessible to CS leaders
- Outcome-focused (time saved, not features)

### Words to Use
- Draft, evidence, sources, citations
- Review, approve, send
- Codebase, repos, docs
- Engineers, teams, customers

### Words to Avoid
- Revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge
- AI-powered (overused, assumed)
- Seamless, frictionless (vague)
- Automate everything (scary, inaccurate)
